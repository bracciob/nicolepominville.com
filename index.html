About Nicole

A background in business analytics. A practice built on relationships.

Originally from Colorado Springs, Nicole holds a BS in Accounting and a Master of Accountancy from the University of Denver. She came to real estate from a career in consulting and recruiting, bringing an analytical rigor and negotiation discipline that shape every transaction.

Today, as a Realtor at Compass, she serves clients across the Denver Metro Area, Colorado Springs, and Northern Colorado through a boutique, referral-driven practice built on discretion, trust, and quiet excellence.
